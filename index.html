👋 Hi, I’m Bani

I am a professional international model based in the UAE, and I was awarded the Best Model in Dubai in 2023.

I've had the opportunity to work with prestigious brands and venues, including: Palazzo Versace Dubai, SLS Hotel, Arjaan by Rotana, Bab Al Shams Hotel, and Anantara Hotel in the UAE.

My extensive experience in fashion shows includes: Dubai Fashion Week, Emirates Fashion Week, SMOD Dubai, Jewellery Fashion Week, World Fashion Festival Awards, Gulf Bride, International Fashion Week Dubai, and events at the American University in the Emirates.

I have collaborated with renowned designers from around the world, including the USA, Italy, France, Russia, Ukraine, the UK, Poland, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Lebanon.

Some notable names are Michael Cinco, Sony Ambassador, and Honor Arabia.

My work has been featured on billboards for brands such as Cavallo Collection, Lozan, and Honor Arabia in Saudi Arabia.

I’ve worked with top jewelry brands like Graff Jewelry, Bayt Damas, Yeprem Jewelry, and Alfardan Jewelry in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Additionally, I’ve collaborated with famous makeup artists, beauty salons, clinics, and numerous restaurants.
